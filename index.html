Research statement
I study the relationship between political violence and democratic inclusion — asking how violence shapes who participates in politics, under what conditions, and with what consequences for democratic representation. A core finding is that political violence produces systematic inequalities along lines of gender, ethnicity, and socioeconomic status. My work draws on original survey data, ethnographic fieldwork, elite interviews, and statistical analysis, with a primary focus on Colombia and Nigeria.

Gender & political violence

How electoral violence shapes candidates' ambition and survival in Colombia.

Gender & conflict resolution

Gender provisions in peace agreements and their implementation. Philippines case study.

Violence & political parties

How parties mediate between violence and participation — as enablers or protectors.

Intersectionality

How gender, ethnicity, and class intersect in shaping vulnerability to political violence.

Research Projects

Ongoing funded projects and international collaborations

01

Violence, Parties & Political Power in Colombia

How violent actors distort the path to political power. Examines party recruitment and electability of candidates with armed-group linkages. Includes survey experiments with party selectors.

Swedish Research Council · 6.7M SEK · 2025–
02

Women's Empowerment in Peace Processes

Building a global dataset on gender provisions in peace agreements (1975–2025) and their implementation. Based on original fieldwork in the Philippines on the Bangsamoro Agreement.

UN Complex Risk Analytics Fund (CRAF)
03

Candidate Survey Colombia (MOE)

Longitudinal large-scale survey of political candidates on campaign experiences and political violence. Covers 2022, 2023, 2026, and 2027 election cycles in partnership with the Electoral Observation Mission.

Swedish Research Council
04

Disasters, Response and Trust in Colombia

Social impacts of climate extremes and their effects on institutional trust in Colombia. New collaborative project with Stefan Döring and Ulrika Lundin Glans.

Climes Seed Grant · 100K SEK · 2026
